# Lead Follow-Up Scripts

Save the text templates below in your phone (iOS: Settings → Keyboard → Text
Replacement; Android: Gboard clipboard or Messages templates).

## First response (SMS, within 5–15 min — send even if you also call)

> "Hi [Name], this is [You] with [Business] — got your request about [service] on your
> place near [town]. I'll take a look at the photos/details and call you
> [today/shortly]. If it's easier, text me 2–3 photos of the area here. Thanks!"

## First call script (keep it 5–8 minutes)

1. **Open:** "Saw your request — tell me about the property and what you want it to
   look like when I'm done."
2. **Qualify (the 6 quoting facts):** What's growing there (brush vs. tree size)?
   Rough size/area? Truck+trailer access? Wet spots or steep ground? Rocks, old fence
   wire, junk hidden in the brush? Timeline?
3. **Set expectations honestly:** "Mulching cuts everything to ground level and leaves
   mulch — it doesn't pull roots or stumps out. On [size] of [vegetation], you're
   typically looking at [half-day/full-day/X days]."
4. **Price frame (protect the number):** Quote a *range* on the call if confident, exact
   number by text/email same day. "Jobs like this usually run $X–$Y. I'll firm it up
   from your photos and send the exact number today."
5. **Close for the next step, always with a date:** "If that works, I've got [day]
   open" or "I'll send the quote tonight and check back Thursday."

## Quote delivery (text + Jobber quote)

> "[Name], here's your quote for [job description]: **$X,XXX** — includes mobilization,
> [scope: e.g., ~2 ac of brush and saplings up to 6″, mulched to grade]. Not included:
> [stumps/roots, material over X″]. I can get you on the schedule for [date window].
> Good for 30 days. Want me to lock that date in?"

## Follow-up cadence (quote sent, no answer)

| When | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Day 2–3 | Text | "Hi [Name] — making sure the quote came through. Any questions on it?" |
| Day 7 | Call, then text | "Scheduling [next 2 weeks] this week — want me to hold [date] for you?" |
| Day 14 | Text (last touch) | "No pressure — if the timing's not right I'll check back in a few weeks. If you're comparing quotes, happy to walk through what's included in mine (insurance, cleanup, no piles left behind)." |
| Day 30 | — | Move to Lost (reason: Ghosted) or a "later" list; one seasonal re-text next quarter: "Getting the fall schedule together — still want that [job]?" |

## Objection quick answers

- **"That's more than I expected."** → "Fair. Most of the cost is real: the machine
  costs more than most houses and this is [X hours] of grinding. Two ways to bring it
  down: shrink the area to the part that matters most, or I can prioritize [key
  section] within a day rate and we see how far we get." *(Scope down, never rate down
  — see the discount rules in `pricing/rate-card.md`.)*
- **"The other guy is cheaper."** → "Ask him three things: is that a purpose-built
  mulcher or a brush hog on a rental, is he insured for this, and what does 'done' look
  like — piles and ruts, or ground you can walk tomorrow?"
- **"Can you do it cheaper if I…"** → Only yes for: flexible scheduling (fill-in work),
  multiple neighbors booking together (shared mobilization), or repeat/annual work.

## Lost lead workflow

Mark Lost + reason immediately (it feeds the dashboard). **Ghosted ≠ Lost-on-price** —
track them separately; they get different re-marketing. Every Lost-on-price lead gets
one message 60–90 days later (the brush grew back on whoever they hired, or they never
did it).
