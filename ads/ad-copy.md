# Ad Copy — Six Angles

> Structure/budgets in [`campaign-plan.md`](campaign-plan.md) — at this budget these six
> "campaigns" run as **ad angles rotated inside one consolidated Leads campaign** (plus
> one retargeting campaign), not six separate campaigns.
>
> Local anchors to use across all copy: "southeast Michigan", "western Wayne, Washtenaw
> & Monroe counties", town names (Belleville, New Boston, Willis, Ypsilanti, Milan,
> Saline, Flat Rock, Carleton).
>
> Insurance confirmed ($400/mo) — "fully insured" is cleared for use in all ads.

## Angle 1 — "Reclaim Your Overgrown Land" (reclaim + before/after + small acreage)

- **Target:** Rural homeowners & small-acreage owners (1–10 ac), 30–65, homeowners, service radius
- **Problem:** Land they can't use or even walk; embarrassment; snakes/ticks; "it got away from us"
- **Offer/angle:** Transformation in a day — show the before/after, no burn piles, no mess left behind
- **Headline:** *"Take Your Land Back — Cleared in a Day"*
- **Primary text:** "That overgrown section you've been avoiding? We turn thick brush, autumn olive, and saplings into clean, walkable ground in a day — no bulldozer, no burn piles, nothing to haul away. The brush gets ground into mulch that protects your soil. Serving western Wayne, Washtenaw & Monroe counties. Send us a couple photos and get a quote, usually same day."
- **CTA button:** Get Quote
- **Destination:** Meta instant form (short version) — highest volume angle; the form converts better than the site for this audience
- **Creative:** Split-screen before/after image; 20–30s vertical video of thick brush → clean pass
- **Lead quality expectation:** High volume, mixed quality — expect tire-kickers with under-minimum jobs; the "approx. size" form question filters
- **Do NOT target:** Renters (exclude via copy: "your land/your property"), dense urban zips, under-25

## Angle 2 — "Hunting Property Prep" (hunting prep + food plots/habitat access)

- **Target:** Hunting-land & deer-property owners; interests: whitetail hunting, food plots, onX Hunt, QDMA/deer management, Mossy Oak, land ownership; men 28–65; widen radius (hunters drive)
- **Problem:** Can't access parts of the property; no shooting lanes; food plot spots locked in brush; want the place ready before season
- **Offer/angle:** Seasonal urgency — "ready by opening day"; talks hunter-to-hunter
- **Headline:** *"Ready Before the October 1 Opener?"*
- **Primary text:** "Shooting lanes, food plot sites, and access trails — cut clean with a forestry mulcher, not ripped up with a dozer. Mulched trails stay walkable and quiet, and deer use the edges. Michigan archery opens October 1 and firearm November 15 — book summer work now so your property is ready, not torn up, when it counts. Tell us what you want opened up and get a quote."
- **CTA:** Get Quote
- **Destination:** Instant form; also run a traffic variant to the Hunting Property page for retargeting
- **Creative:** Trail-cut POV video; food-plot-edge before/after; drone line of a cut shooting lane
- **Lead quality:** Best-fit leads — hunters plan ahead, own acreage, pay for results; often repeat annual customers
- **Do NOT target:** Public-land-only hunters (copy self-selects: "your property"); don't waste budget on this angle Nov–Jan

## Angle 3 — "Clear Brush Without Tearing Up the Property" (mulching vs. dozer education)

- **Target:** Rural homeowners, farmers, pond owners who *think* their only option is a bulldozer; slightly older skew 35–70
- **Problem:** Want it cleared but afraid of mud pits, ripped topsoil, root balls, burn piles, big-contractor mess
- **Offer/angle:** Education — mulching is the clean alternative; positions you before they call a dozer guy
- **Headline:** *"There's a Cleaner Way to Clear Land Than a Bulldozer"*
- **Primary text:** "A dozer rips everything out — roots, topsoil, and all — and leaves you with piles to burn or haul. Forestry mulching grinds brush and small trees in place, leaves the ground covered and stable, and it's done in one visit with one machine. See the difference in the video, then send photos of your property for a quote."
- **CTA:** Learn More → Forestry Mulching page (the education angle suits the website), form on page
- **Creative:** Side-by-side "dozer mess vs. mulched finish"; talking-head-over-footage explainer (30–45s)
- **Lead quality:** Fewer, better-educated leads; good average job size
- **Do NOT target:** People needing full site prep/grubbing for construction pads (they actually need a dozer — say so in the FAQ; saves everyone time)

## Angle 4 — "Fence Line & Property Line Cleanup"

- **Target:** Farmers, pasture owners, rural homeowners with acreage; interests: cattle, farming, tractor brands, rural living
- **Problem:** Fence rows swallowed by brush/cedar/hedge; can't inspect or repair fence; neighbors/surveys need the line opened
- **Headline:** *"See Your Fence Line Again"*
- **Primary text:** "Brush, cedars, and saplings eating your fence rows? We mulch fence lines clean without pulling the fence down or leaving piles — you get an inspectable, maintainable line in a day. Per-line pricing, fast quotes from a few photos."
- **CTA:** Get Quote → instant form
- **Creative:** Long tracking shot down a half-cleared fence line (the most satisfying possible footage); before/after stills
- **Lead quality:** High — clearly scoped jobs, easy to quote by the foot/hour, farmers refer other farmers
- **Do NOT target:** Suburban lots (no fence rows)

## Angle 5 — "Trails & Access Paths"

- **Target:** Larger-parcel owners (5+ ac), hobby farms, recreational land, ATV/UTV interest overlays
- **Problem:** Own land they can't get across; want riding/walking trails, access to the back of the property, creek access
- **Headline:** *"Open Up Trails Through Your Own Land"*
- **Primary text:** "It's your property — you should be able to reach all of it. We cut clean, mulched trails and access paths sized for your UTV, tractor, or truck. No ruts, no piles, usable the same day. Tell us roughly where you want to go and we'll quote it."
- **CTA:** Get Quote → instant form
- **Creative:** POV ride-through of a finished trail (GoPro on UTV); overhead drone of a trail snaking through woods
- **Lead quality:** Good; trail jobs are fast, high-margin, photogenic (feeds the gallery)
- **Do NOT target:** Public trail / municipal audiences

## Angle 6 — "Professional Equipment, Fast Response" (credibility/retargeting)

- **Target:** RETARGETING ONLY — video viewers (50%+), page engagers, site visitors, form-openers who didn't submit (past 30–60 days)
- **Problem:** Saw you, didn't act; needs a trust nudge and a reason now
- **Headline:** *"Still Looking at That Overgrown Spot? Quotes Are Free."*
- **Primary text:** "Owner-operated, fully insured, purpose-built Cat forestry mulcher — not a rental with a brush hog. You talk directly to the person running the machine. Send 2–3 photos, get a real number, usually same day. [Season note: summer/fall schedule is filling.]"
- **CTA:** Get Quote → instant form
- **Creative:** Equipment beauty shot + your face; 15s "here's how a quote works" clip; review screenshot once available
- **Lead quality:** Highest close rate of any angle — warm audience
- **Do NOT target:** Anyone who already submitted (exclude the leads custom audience)

## Copy rules across all ads

- Lead with the customer's land, never the machine specs ("Cat 275XE" means nothing to
  them; "no torn-up ground" means everything).
- Always say what happens next: photos → quote → one-day job.
- Local anchor in every ad: "[County/Region] forestry mulching."
- One idea per ad; before/after is the universal proof.
- Never promise stump/root removal — mulching cuts to grade, it doesn't grub.
