# Photo / Video Shot List

> **Shot #2 (anchored before/after pairs) is partially covered already** — existing
> before/after photos live in the "broadmark photos" file (Downloads folder). See
> [`creative-library.md`](creative-library.md) for the catalog and what's still missing.

**Gear needed:** your phone (shoot 4K, horizontal AND vertical takes), a $30 phone
tripod, a GoPro or phone chest/cab mount (nice-to-have), and ideally a $300-ish drone or
a friend with one (nice-to-have later, not a blocker). Golden hour (first/last 2 hours
of sun) doubles the quality of every outdoor shot for free.

| # | Shot | What to capture | Why it matters | Where it's used | Copy it supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero action | 275XE+HM418 mid-mulch, low angle, chips flying, shot from 45° front-side, golden hour | The single money shot — power + professionalism | Website hero, page covers, retargeting ads | "Professional equipment, clean work" |
| 2 | Anchored before/after pairs | Same spot, same angle, same height — take the "before" EVERY job before unloading (make it a checklist habit); mark your feet position with a stake or drop a pin | Paired proof is the highest-converting creative in this niche | Gallery, Reclaim + Mulching-vs-Dozer ads, every service page | "Reclaim your land", "cleared in a day" |
| 3 | Timelapse clear | Phone on tripod at property corner, full area clear compressed to 30–60s | Shows "a day" is literal | Reels/vertical ads, homepage | "Done in one visit" |
| 4 | Trail POV | GoPro/phone ride-through of finished trail on UTV or walking; slow, steady | Sells the *feeling* of access | Trails ads, hunting page | "Open trails through your own land" |
| 5 | Fence line tracking shot | Walk or drive parallel to a half-done fence row: cleared half vs. jungle half in one frame | One frame = the entire value proposition | Fence line ads | "See your fence line again" |
| 6 | Shooting lane / food plot reveal | Stand at the stand/blind location: before (wall of brush) vs. after (open lane); drone down-the-lane if possible | Hunter-specific proof | Hunting ads, hunting page | "Ready before opening day" |
| 7 | Mulcher close-up | Slow-mo (240fps phone) of the HM418 drum feeding a 4–6″ sapling; teeth detail while parked | Mesmerizing = watch time = cheaper ads; also shows capability limits honestly | Reels, Mulching-vs-Dozer ads | "Grinds brush in place" |
| 8 | Finished-ground walk | You walking across fresh mulch, kneel, grab a handful: "this is what's left — no ruts, no piles" | Answers the #1 unspoken question: what's left behind? | Mulching-vs-Dozer ads, FAQ, quote follow-up texts | "Without tearing up the property" |
| 9 | Operator intro | 20–30s: you in front of the machine — name, area served, "owner-operated, insured, I answer my own phone" | Faces close local deals; differentiates from faceless outfits | About page, retargeting, quote follow-ups | Trust/credibility |
| 10 | Customer-style walkthrough | After a job, film the walk with the happy customer if willing (or you narrating their reaction); even an audio-only quote over footage works | Social proof without needing reviews yet | Retargeting, gallery captions | "Here's what [Name] in [Town] had us do" |
| 11 | Rig credibility shot | Truck + trailer + machine loaded, clean, at a property gate, morning light | "This is a real business" in one image | Service Area page, About, retargeting | "Professional equipment, fast response" |
| 12 | Cab POV | Phone mounted in cab: operator view of a pass through heavy brush, 15–30s | Native-feeling Reels content; cheap reach | Organic FB/IG, ad variants | Any angle |
| 13 | Drone orbit/reveal (later) | Orbit of a cleared parcel; slow reveal from woods to opened land | Scale — makes 3 acres look like an achievement | Hero video, Reclaim ads | "Land transformation" |
| 14 | Problem shots | Pure "before" misery: ticks-and-snakes brush walls, swallowed fences, unusable lots | Ad hooks — the first 2 seconds that stop the scroll | First frame of video ads | "That section you've been avoiding" |

**Standing rule:** every job produces minimum 1 before/after pair + 1 vertical video
clip. Ten jobs in, you have a content library most competitors never build. Get a
one-line photo release into your quote terms ("we may photograph work for our portfolio
— no address shown").
