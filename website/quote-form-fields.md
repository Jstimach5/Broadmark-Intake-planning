# Quote Form Fields

## Website form (long version — Jobber request form)

Keep it to ~60 seconds to complete. Required fields marked *.

1. **Name***
2. **Phone*** (mobile — tell them you'll text the quote)
3. Email
4. **Property address or nearest town*** (address preferred — enables travel pricing)
5. **Service needed*** (dropdown): Brush/overgrowth clearing • Trails or access paths •
   Hunting property (food plots/shooting lanes) • Fence line clearing • Lot clearing •
   Pond/field edge • Pasture reclamation • Other
6. **Approximate size*** (dropdown, not free text): Under ½ acre • ½–1 acre • 1–3 acres •
   3–5 acres • 5–10 acres • 10+ acres • "A strip/trail — length in notes"
7. Vegetation type (dropdown): Light brush/weeds • Heavy brush/saplings • Small trees up
   to ~6″ • Larger trees mixed in • Not sure — photos attached
8. Equipment access: Can a truck & trailer reach the work area? (Yes / Close — short
   walk-in / Difficult / Not sure)
9. Ground conditions: Any wet, swampy, or steep areas? (No / Some / Yes / Not sure)
10. Timeline (dropdown): ASAP • Within 2–4 weeks • 1–3 months • Just planning/pricing
11. Photo upload (2–3 photos; "even phone pics from the property line help us quote faster")
12. How did you hear about us? (Facebook • Google • Referral • Saw us working • Other)
13. Anything else we should know? (free text)

## Meta instant form (short version — inside Facebook lead ads)

Fewer fields = more leads; the phone call fills the gaps. Every field beyond ~4 adds
roughly 5–10% to cost per lead but raises quality.

1. Name (prefilled by Meta)
2. Phone (prefilled)
3. City/Town
4. Service needed (multiple choice — same options as above)
5. Approximate size (multiple choice)
6. Timeline (multiple choice)

**Settings:** start with the **"More volume"** form type; switch to **"Higher intent"**
(adds a review screen before submit) if the junk rate gets high. Privacy policy URL is
mandatory. Thank-you screen message:

> "Thanks [Name] — I'll call you shortly from [number]. Text photos of the area to that
> number to speed up your quote."
