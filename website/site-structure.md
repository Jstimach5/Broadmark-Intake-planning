# Website Structure

**Principle:** the entire site exists to do two things — build trust in under 10 seconds
and get the visitor to submit the quote form or call. One page could do it; a small
multi-page site wins for local SEO ("forestry mulching near [town]") and gives ads
distinct landing pages.

**Platform:** any mainstream builder **on a tier that allows embed code** (needed for the
Jobber request form script + Meta pixel) — Squarespace Business, Wix, or WordPress with a
lightweight theme; ~$15–30/mo. The quote form on every page is Jobber's embeddable
request form, so website leads land in Jobber natively (and trigger its push
notification) with zero middleware. The site must also host a **privacy policy page** —
a hard requirement before Meta will publish a lead form.

## Pages

| About | Trust | Owner/operator story, photo of you with the machine, insured statement |
| Contact | Catch-all | Phone (tap-to-call), form, service area recap |

## Homepage — section by section

1. **Hero** — Best action photo of the 275XE/HM418 mid-mulch (or 5–10s looping video).
   Headline is the outcome, not the equipment. Two buttons: **Get a Fast Quote** (form)
   and **Call/Text [number]**.
2. **Main offer strip** — one sentence: free quotes, most within 24 hours, most jobs
   done in 1–2 days.
3. **Trust bar** — Licensed & insured • Owner-operated •
   Sumpter Township-based • Google review
   stars (once you have 5+) • "You talk to the person running the machine."
4. **Services grid** — 6 cards with photo + 1-liner: Brush & Overgrowth Clearing,
   Trails & Access Paths, Hunting Property (food plots/shooting lanes), Fence Line
   Clearing, Lot & Small Acreage Clearing, Pond & Field Edge Cleanup. Each links to its
   service page.
5. **Before/After** — 3–4 slider pairs or side-by-sides. Captions like "2 acres of
   overgrown pasture — 1 day."
6. **Why mulching / equipment** — photo of the Cat 275XE + HM418 with 3 bullets: no burn
   piles or hauling; ground left mulched (erosion control, walkable immediately);
   lighter footprint than dozers — no torn-up topsoil or ripped-out root balls.
7. **Process** — 4 steps: ① send photos & details → ② fast quote → ③ we schedule →
   ④ done, walk-through, invoice. Kills the fear of the "contractor black hole."
8. **Service area** — map graphic + towns list. "Based in Sumpter Township. Serving
   within ~25 miles; farther for full-day+ jobs."
9. **Quote form** — the full Jobber form embedded (don't make them click away).
10. **FAQ** — 6–8 questions (drafted in `homepage-copy.md`).
11. **Final CTA** — repeat hero CTA + phone number.
