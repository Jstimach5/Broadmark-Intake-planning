## Rate card

Anchored to verified market: $150–300/hr national; published operators at $200/hr + 3-hr
minimum and $250/hr + half-day minimum; day rates $1,500–2,500.

| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum job** | **$1,200** (≈3 hrs + mobilization) | Below this, jobs don't cover the trailer move. Real operators publish $600–$2,100 minimums; $1,200 is mid-market |
| **Mobilization** | $150 flat within ~25 mi; +$3–4/loaded mile beyond | Market norm $150–300. Waive only when bundling neighbors |
| **Hourly** | **$225/hr** (3-hr min) | For odd/unclear scopes. A 40-gpm high-flow rig is top of the CTL class — don't price like a rental Bobcat ($160 floor) |
| **Half-day (4 hrs on site)** | **$950** | Effective ~$237/hr — small jobs pay a premium |
| **Full day (8 hrs)** | **$1,700** | Effective ~$212/hr — the bread-and-butter quote unit |
| **Multi-day** | **$1,600/day** (2+ days) | The discount comes from saved mobilization, never from cutting the rate |
| Per-acre — light brush | $650–900/ac (3 ac min) | Only when photos/walk verify uniform light cover; production 2–4 ac/day |
| Per-acre — medium (brush + trees ≤6″) | $1,200–1,800/ac | Production 1–2 ac/day. Cross-check: never quote per-acre below the day-rate equivalent |
| Per-acre — heavy (dense, 6–8″ stems) | **Don't.** Quote as estimated days | Production 0.5–1 ac/day; per-acre risk is all yours |

## Surcharges (stack them — they reflect real cost)

- **Steep or rocky ground: +25–50%** (rock cuts tooth life 10×; industry-standard surcharge)
- **Heavy material (frequent 6–8″ stems): +25%** — the HM418 is rated to 8″ but the sweet
  spot is ≤6″; 8″+ stems get chainsaw-felled first, billed T&M, or declined
- **Wet ground:** reschedule by default; +25% if the customer insists and it's workable
- **Travel:** beyond ~25 mi, per-mile mobilization; beyond ~60 mi, full-day jobs only
- **Hidden wire/junk discovered mid-job:** stop, photo, change-order at the hourly rate —
  put "site must be disclosed; concealed metal/rock billed hourly" in quote terms

## Add-ons (upsell menu on every quote)

- Finish/back-drag second pass for a lawn-ready look: +10–15%
- Chainsaw pre-fell of oversize trees: T&M ($125/hr labor)
- **Regrowth maintenance plan: $500–1,500/visit, annually or every other year** —
  mulching doesn't kill roots; regrowth in 6–12 months is guaranteed. This is the
  recurring-revenue engine: pitch it AT the walkthrough, not later
- Herbicide follow-up: refer/partner unless you get licensed (state applicator license required)

## Discount rules

**Allowed (structural discounts — cost actually drops):**
- Multi-day (saved mobilization)
- Neighbor bundling (2+ properties same trip: waive the second mobilization)
- Fill-in scheduling ("I can flex you into a cancellation for 10% off")
- Repeat/annual maintenance customers

**Never:**
- Matching a brush-hog or rental-machine quote (different product — say so)
- Cash-discount pressure
- "The other guy is cheaper" without a written scope match
- Anything that takes the effective rate below **$175/hr** (the walk-away floor;
  $150 is break-even)

## Red flags — decline or hourly-only

Rock outcrops visible • old interior fence lines buried in brush • dumped
junk/appliances in the vegetation • majority of stems >8″ • standing water/swamp •
slopes that feel sketchy on the walk • customer wants "roots gone / build-ready pad"
(that's grubbing — wrong tool, refer it out) • price-only shoppers at bush-hog budgets •
under-minimum jobs 45+ minutes away • "just bill me whatever it takes" with no scope
agreement.
